Save any public Reel to your phone.

Paste the link, tap download, get an MP4. No login, no app, no watermark added by us.

Works with public Reels, videos and posts. Private accounts can't be downloaded.

How to save a Reel

  1. Copy the linkIn Instagram, open the Reel, tap the share icon and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste it aboveTap Paste (or long-press the box) and press Get video.
  3. Download the MP4Tap Download. On iPhone the file lands in Files; on Android it appears in your Gallery.

Original quality

You get the best quality Instagram makes publicly available, up to 1080p.

Nothing stored

We don't keep links, files or download history.

Any device

It runs in your browser on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no daily limit beyond basic fair-use protection.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Only public content can be fetched, and we never ask for your Instagram password.

Where does the video go on iPhone?

Safari saves it to the Files app. Press and hold the file and choose Save to Photos.

Can I repost what I download?

Only with the creator's permission. Download for personal, offline viewing and credit the creator when you share.
